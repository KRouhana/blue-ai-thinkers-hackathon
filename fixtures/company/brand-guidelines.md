# Brand and component guidelines

Primary actions use the blue palette token. Destructive actions use red. Neutral is the default for
secondary buttons, and amber is reserved for warnings about synthetic or incomplete data.

Button labels are two to three words in sentence case. Avoid exclamation marks and avoid the word
"Click". A label longer than three words is a sign the button is doing too much.

Pill radius is reserved for marketing pages. Product surfaces use the medium radius token so that
buttons, inputs, and cards agree. Square corners are used only inside dense tables.

Sizes come from the size tokens: small for inline actions, medium for form submits, large for a page's
single most important action, and extra large only on the marketing hero.

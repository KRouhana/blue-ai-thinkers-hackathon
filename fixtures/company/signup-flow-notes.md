# Signup flow notes

The signup page must remain usable without an account. Anything that requires signing in belongs
behind the trial, not in front of it. Demos should never depend on a real authentication provider.

"Start trial" is the primary call to action on the signup route and should be the largest control on
the page. It is the one button we are willing to make louder.

"Explore sample" opens a read-only sample workspace filled with synthetic data. It is a secondary
action: it should stay visually quieter than Start trial, and its records are labelled as examples.

Pricing and billing copy is out of scope for prototypes. Use the sample workspace instead of any
payment step, and say plainly that payments are simulated.
